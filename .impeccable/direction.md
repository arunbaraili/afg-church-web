# Gathering hall — approved

Mode: Persuade. User selected the gathering hall direction and code-first execution on the local decision page.

Seven grounded candidates considered: welcome table (hospitality), Sunday bulletin (publication), family album (community archive), Scripture reading (typography), gathering hall (place; assigned and selected), woven gathering (textile), neighborhood wayfinding (navigation).

Warm plaster cream, forest green, restrained terracotta, timber tones from real church photos. Large editorial serif headlines paired with a highly readable sans serif. Equal content edges and open spacing; practical visitor information remains immediately accessible. This is a church, with no invented programs, claims or imagery.

FIRST VIEWPORT: quiet masthead; a large welcome on the left, one strong church photograph on the right, Sunday service time and an obvious visit action. Mobile stacks content with the action visible early.

Visitor path: welcome → Sunday details → Mission and Scripture → About and community photographs → pastors and leaders → contact and directions. Mission stories appear when editors add them. Original biographies are retained in content, but unfinished ones are not publicly advertised.

Signature interaction: the mission Scripture gains subtle word emphasis while scrolling, with readable static text without JavaScript or under reduced motion. Community images gently zoom only when linked. Buttons give a short hover and press response. No scroll interception, carousel or autoplay video.

Named raises from declined directions: theatre poster — immediate Sunday invitation; ornament program — consistent proportions; deep dive — clear visitor path; neon circuit — continuous interaction paths; floating pigment — connected composition; meter bridge — aligned service facts. These transfer discipline, not their decorative motifs.

Cross-surface use: the same masthead, type, container, form fields and footer support policy and future content pages. Honest risk: real legacy media has mixed lighting and aspect ratios; crop deliberately without misrepresenting people.

Owner constraints override Taste defaults: preserve real full street address, phone and email; preserve useful leadership labels; no invented .example contacts, hours, ministries, or paid services. Impeccable direction owns palette and layout where its workflow conflicts with Taste's random dealer. Natural content height takes precedence over forcing sparse content into whole-screen blocks.
