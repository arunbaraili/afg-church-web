# AFG Nepali Church

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro and TypeScript for a static, progressively enhanced website. Decap CMS remains the editor; GitHub stores content and Netlify remains the intended host. No additional paid service.

## Users

Visitors and members of Assembly Faith Gospel Nepali Church in Nashville, including the Nepali community and neighbors from all backgrounds. Church editors need to update text, mission content, photos, service information, and leadership without editing code.

## Product Purpose

Introduce the church, explain its mission, help people find Sunday worship, and provide a way to connect.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Rebuild Home, Mission, About Us, and Connect. Preserve existing leaders, privacy content, church details, social links, Formspree destination, and real media. Organize sections as separate components and CMS records. Keep all work local and uncommitted until the owner reviews the preview. Existing domain: afgchurch.com. Cost preference: no additional subscriptions. Production CMS authentication must be verified before deployment; the incumbent Git Gateway is deprecated.

## Evidence on Hand

The original home.json, content/privacy.md, Cloudinary leadership photos, the existing public gallery endpoint, and uploads/background-video/churchVid.mp4. Mission title exists but its items are empty; do not invent ministry programs or theological statements. Placeholder leadership biographies and conflicting contact emails require owner review; preserve factual records and avoid advertising unfinished bios.

## Product Principles

- Church identity and real community lead the experience.
- Service details are easy to find on phones.
- Essential content works without client JavaScript.
- Editors can manage the requested sections and images.
- No changes to production or Git history before local review.
